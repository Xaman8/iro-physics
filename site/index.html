Inverted Reliability Ontology

Can a failure in one envelope be predicted from energy that arrives from another, and how far is the cause?

The bench apparatus is a toy plant. Assets exchange power through ports: an effort and a flow, one pair for each domain. Couplings are the links, source port to target port. Envelopes group the assets, and each envelope reduces to one equivalent asset whose ports sit on the boundary. Entropy generation is the damage proxy.

The headline test is a pump pulsation in envelope A. The power crosses the boundary and heat exchanger HX1, in envelope B, reaches its limit. The drawings below are the plant. The numbers under them are counts from this plant and from the study files.
